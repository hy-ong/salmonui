---
title: Range calendar with two months
description: RangeCalendar with two visible months, hover and focus preview, minDays and maxDays, a disabled day that blocks the range, and the getRangeDays helper.
category: calendars
---

After the first click the days outside the allowed span are disabled and
the range previews under the pointer or the keyboard focus. A live region
announces the start, then the complete range with its day count.
