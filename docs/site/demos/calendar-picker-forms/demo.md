---
title: Pickers in a form
description: Pickers post ISO values, take part in required and range validation, and restore their default values on reset.
category: calendars
---

Submit to see the posted `FormData`: ISO dates, an ISO date-time, an ISO
interval and an ISO time, whatever the display locale. Clear "Due" and
submit: the browser's required message appears and focus moves to the first
segment. Reset restores every default. The sheet picker always opens as a
modal bottom sheet (`popupMode="modal"`).
