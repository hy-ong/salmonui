---
title: PDF viewer
description: A PDF document with the bookmarks panel, thumbnails, zoom, find, text markup from the selection, a fillable form field, print and download.
category: editors
---

Open a bookmark or a thumbnail, zoom with the toolbar or Ctrl+= / Ctrl+-,
and press Ctrl+F to find "salmon". Select text and press Highlight,
Underline or Strikethrough to mark it; page 3 has a form field you can fill.
Download saves a PDF with your marks and form values written into it. The
event log shows the callbacks.
