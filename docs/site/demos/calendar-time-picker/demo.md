---
title: TimePicker
description: A time list every 30 minutes inside a window with a disabled lunch hour, custom options, and a windowed list of all 1,440 minutes.
category: calendars
---

Open a list with Alt+ArrowDown or the clock button; Up / Down move the
active option while focus stays in the field. The third picker lists every
minute of the day: only the visible options are in the DOM.
