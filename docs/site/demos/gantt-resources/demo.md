title: Gantt resources and split tasks
description: People assigned with units, the resource view with an Unassigned row, over-allocated ranges, overlapping bars stacked in lanes, multi-taskbar rows, a task split into segments with split and merge, and taskbar drags that reassign people.
category: calendars
---

The resource view lists each person with their tasks, then an Unassigned
row for "Label boxes". Ana works on the archive at 100 % and on the network
plan at 50 % at the same time: the over-allocated days are marked in her
row. Overlapping bars stack in lanes (untick "Stack" to let them overlap),
and the person rows show their tasks' bars (`multiTaskbar`); collapse a row
to keep its bars. Drag a bar onto another person's row to reassign it, or
onto Unassigned to remove the assignment. Untick "Resource view" for the
project view, where the resources column shows the assignments and the
dialog has a Resources tab. Cabling is split into two segments around the
inspection; drag a segment, right-click a bar for Split task / Merge task,
or use the buttons.
