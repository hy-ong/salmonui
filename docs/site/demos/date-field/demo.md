---
title: Segmented date fields
description: DateField in the locale's segment order and digits, a pattern format with extra paste formats, strict bounds with an error message, and a date-time field in a fixed time zone.
category: calendars
---

Focus a segment and type digits (the field advances when a segment is
complete), press Up / Down to step, or paste `2026-12-01` or a date in the
locale's order. The first field posts its ISO value under `name="start"`.
