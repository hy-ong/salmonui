---
title: Tree View drag and drop
description: Two task trees in one drag group with pointer moves before, after or into an item, Alt+arrow keyboard moves and F2 rename, applied to application data with moveTreeItems.
category: navigation
---

Drag a task onto the top or bottom edge of another to reorder it, onto its
middle to nest it, or across to the other list. With a task focused,
Alt+arrows move it from the keyboard and F2 renames it.
