---
title: Right-to-left
description: An RTL grid with 30 virtualized columns - mirrored layout and pinned zones, flipped arrow keys, mirrored resize handles and drag indicators, and normalized horizontal scrolling.
---

The first column is pinned to the start, which is the right edge in RTL, and
the last column to the end. Drag a header's left edge to resize it, drag a
header to reorder it, or focus a header and press Ctrl+Alt+ArrowLeft to widen
it: in RTL the column end is on the left.
