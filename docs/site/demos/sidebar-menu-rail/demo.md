---
title: Sidebar menu icon rail
description: A SidebarMenu inside a SidebarLayout that collapses to a 64px icon rail; links show label tooltips and submenus open as flyouts on hover, click or the keyboard.
category: navigation
---

Press the collapse button to shrink the panel to its rail. Pass the layout's
`collapsed` state to the menu: labels hide (they stay the accessible names),
hovering or keyboard-focusing a link shows its label as a tooltip, and a
submenu opens as a flyout next to its button. Hover opens it for a quick
look; a click, Enter, Space or Right pins it and moves focus into it. Escape
or Left returns to the button, and Tab walks through the flyout and on to
the next rail item.
