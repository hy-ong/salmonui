---
title: TreeSelect basics
description: A controlled single TreeSelect where only leaves are selectable, a searchable flat-data TreeSelect whose matches keep their ancestors, and a required form field.
category: dropdowns
---

Open the **Office** picker: the selected office's region is already
expanded and in view. Regions and countries are not selectable, so a click
on one expands or collapses it; Arrow Right and Left do the same from the
keyboard. Type in the **Department** search: matches stay under their
parents. Submitting the empty form shows the browser's own required message.
