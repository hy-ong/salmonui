---
title: Theme colors and contrast
description: The five semantic color seeds and the color scheme are the whole theme input; the derived palette keeps text, border, focus and selection contrast for any seed, checked live below the grid.
---

Pick any seed: surfaces, borders, text, hover, selection and focus colors are
derived from it, and the table reads the derived `--salmon-*` variables and
checks the contrast guarantees. There are no theme presets and no Theme
Studio; the seeds replace both.
