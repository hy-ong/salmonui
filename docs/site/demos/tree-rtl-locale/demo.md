---
title: Tree RTL, locale and adaptive
description: An office tree in right-to-left mode with mirrored indentation, chevrons and keys, German localeText for the tree strings, locale number, currency and date formats, a lookup column and validation while editing, and adaptive cards that show the toggle and the level.
---

`dir="rtl"` mirrors the indentation (logical padding), the chevron, the
ArrowRight / ArrowLeft tree keys and the indent keys. German `localeText`
covers the tree keys (`treeExpand`, `treeCollapse`, `treeExpanded`,
`treeCollapsed`, `treeLoading`, `treeLoadError`, `treeRetry`, `treeLevel`,
`indent`, `outdent`) next to the rest of the grid strings, and `locale`
formats revenue and dates. Turn on adaptive cards: each card shows the
toggle and "Ebene n" / "Level n" instead of the indentation.
