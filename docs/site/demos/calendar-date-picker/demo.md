---
title: DatePicker
description: A date field with a popup calendar, bounds, disabled weekends, a month picker and the ISO value it reports.
category: calendars
---

Type into the segments, or press Alt+ArrowDown (or the calendar button) and
pick a day. Weekends are disabled; the month picker selects from the year
view. The panel shows the `PlainDate` and the `details.iso` of the last
change.
