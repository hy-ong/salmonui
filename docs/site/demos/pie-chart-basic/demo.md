---
title: Doughnut with legend and selection
description: A doughnut chart with a center label that follows the hovered slice, a legend toolbar that hides slices, selection, explosion and outside labels.
category: data-visualization
---

Click a slice to select and explode it; the legend buttons hide slices
(`hiddenPoints`). Tab to the chart and use the arrow keys: each slice is
announced with its value and percent.
