---
title: Date and time
description: `DatePicker granularity="minute"` with a 15-minute time list inside office hours and a time zone.
category: calendars
---

Pick a day: focus moves to the time part. The list runs from 09:00 to 17:00
every 15 minutes; on 10 November it starts at 10:30 (`min`). The value is
read and reported in Europe/Berlin, so `details.iso` carries the offset.
