---
title: Color picker parts
description: ColorArea, hue and alpha ColorSlider, ColorField, ColorSwatch and ColorSwatchPicker composed by hand around one Color value.
category: inputs
---

Every part of the color picker is exported on its own. Drag the area or
the sliders, or use the arrow keys (Shift for large steps); type any hex,
`rgb()` or `hsl()` text in the field.
