---
title: Keyboard and screen reader
description: One labelled `role="grid"` tree over 2,000 virtualized rows - the keyboard map, a single tab stop, live-region announcements mirrored below the grid, and focus that survives virtualization and menus.
---

Click a cell or Tab into the grid, then use the arrow keys, Home / End,
Ctrl+Home / Ctrl+End and PageUp / PageDown. On a header, Enter sorts,
Shift+Alt+ArrowDown opens the column menu and Escape closes it. Space
toggles the row selection. Every message the grid sends to its live region
is copied into the announcement log.
