---
title: Mention
description: A textarea with @ people and # tags - suggestions at the caret, keyboard selection, highlighted matches, a disabled person and accent-insensitive search.
category: dropdowns
---

Type `@` to list people or `#` to list tags, then keep typing to filter:
"jose" finds "José García". Arrow keys move through the list (Dara is
disabled and skipped), Enter or Tab inserts, Escape closes. People are
inserted without the `@`; tags keep their `#`. Ctrl+Z undoes an insertion.
