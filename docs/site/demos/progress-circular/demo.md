---
title: Circular and semicircle progress
description: Circular Progress in three sizes and a pixel diameter with a custom thickness, a buffer, segments, an indeterminate spinner, centered labels and a custom center node, semicircle gauges and a Meter.
category: notifications
---

`size` takes `'sm'`, `'md'` or `'lg'`, or a diameter in pixels (the
storage ring is 120 px with a 14 px `thickness`); `style` adds its margin.
The storage ring centers a node in the ring, the battery gauge is `shape="semicircle"`, and
the Meter is a measured value (`role="meter"`) with a range-color tone.
