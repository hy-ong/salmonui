---
title: Context Menu
description: A ContextMenu on a canvas whose entries change with the right-clicked shape, with a submenu, a disabled item and a vetoed target that keeps the browser menu.
category: navigation
---

Right-click the canvas (long-press on touch, or focus it and press
Shift+F10). A shape adds its own **Delete** item; the "Browser menu" label
keeps the browser's own menu.
