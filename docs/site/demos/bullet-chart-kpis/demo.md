---
title: Bullet chart KPIs
description: Quarterly KPIs as a BulletChart with three qualitative bands, a target and a stretch target, value labels, a legend, a custom tooltip and click events; switch orientation, feature glyph, axis side and animation.
category: data-visualization
---

Each KPI is one item: the dark bar (or dot) is the actual value, the line
is the target and the cross the stretch target, drawn over the Poor,
Satisfactory and Good bands. Tab to the chart and use the arrow keys: the
live region reads the value, both targets and the band. Click an item to
log it. "Show Q4" changes the data, so the bars transition; turn off
"Animate" and they jump.
