---
title: Multiple dates, bounds and outside days
description: selectionMode="multiple" with a controlled array of dates, min and max bounds, Monday as the first day of the week, narrow weekday headers and visible outside days.
category: calendars
---

Click or press Enter on a day to toggle it. The previous and next buttons
stop at the bounds and keyboard focus is clamped to them. The value is a
plain array: there are no `addDate()` / `removeDate()` methods.
