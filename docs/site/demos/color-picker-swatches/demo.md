---
title: Color picker inline palette
description: An inline ColorPicker in swatches mode with a configurable number of columns and custom swatches from renderSwatch.
category: inputs
---

The panel renders in place (`inline`): no trigger and no popover. Change
"Columns" to reflow the palette, and tick "Custom swatches" to draw each
swatch with `renderSwatch` (a rounded chip with a check mark on the
selected color). Tab into the palette, then move with the arrow keys and
pick with Enter or Space.
