---
title: Scheduler timeline and resources
description: Rooms and their people as timeline rows with resource colors and work hours, plus Timeline Year, Year, Agenda and Month Agenda views.
category: calendars
---

Each room is a parent row with its people below; collapse a room with its
arrow. Events take the room color, a double click on a cell creates an event
for that person, and the view buttons switch to the day, month and year
timelines and to the year and agenda views.
