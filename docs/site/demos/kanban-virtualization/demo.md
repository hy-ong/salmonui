---
title: Kanban with 10,000 cards
description: Five columns of 2,000 cards each with per-column virtualization, measured variable card heights, drag auto-scroll and focusCard through the ref API.
category: data-visualization
---

Each column renders only the cards in view and measures their heights as
they appear. Drag a card toward the bottom of a column to auto-scroll it.
**Go to card** scrolls the card's column and moves focus to it. Narrow the
window below 600 px of board width to see the one-column layout with its
column picker.
