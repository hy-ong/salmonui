---
title: Listbox grid layout
description: A selectable list whose rows hold their own buttons, and a four-column tile gallery, both on the APG Grid pattern of Listbox layout="grid".
category: dropdowns
---

Tab into the messages list, move with Up and Down, then press Tab or Right
Arrow to reach the row's star button; Escape returns to the row. In the
gallery the arrow keys move in two dimensions and Enter or Space picks a
swatch. In both, selection does not follow focus.
