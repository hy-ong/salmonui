---
title: Dual list
description: Two reorderable Listboxes in one drag group with move selected, move all, move up and move down buttons built on moveItems and reorderItems; Alt+Up / Alt+Down reorder from the keyboard.
category: dropdowns
---

Select items and use the buttons, press Alt+Up / Alt+Down to move the
selection within a list (announced in a live region), or drag items within a
list and between the two lists. Escape cancels a drag.
