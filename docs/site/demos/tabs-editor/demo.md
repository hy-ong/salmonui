---
title: Closable, reorderable tabs with overflow
description: An editor-style tab strip with close buttons, Delete to close, drag or Alt+Arrow to reorder, and overflow into a More tabs menu or a scrolling strip.
category: navigation
---

Close a tab with its × or by pressing Delete on it. Drag a tab, or press
Alt+ArrowLeft / Alt+ArrowRight, to move it. With `overflow="menu"` the tabs
that do not fit go into the **More tabs** menu, and the selected tab always
stays in the strip.
