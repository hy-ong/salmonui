---
title: Large tree
description: A 28,208-row sales tree with 24 month columns under stacked year headers, row and column virtualization, cascading checkboxes, batch editing across scrolled-out rows, and resize, reorder, pin, column menu and chooser on a tree.
---

Rows and the unpinned columns are virtualized over the tree view, so expand
all and scrolling stay fast. Virtualization does not turn other features
off: checkboxes with a cascade, batch editing (drafts are kept by row id
while their rows are scrolled away) and stacked headers work with row and
column virtualization. The tree column is pinned and keeps its toggle while
the month columns scroll; move, hide or unpin it and the tree moves with it.
