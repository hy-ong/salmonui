---
title: Carousel auto-play
description: A fading Carousel that rotates automatically, with a play/pause button, a per-slide interval, a progress indicator, hover-only arrows and CarouselApi calls.
category: navigation
---

Rotation pauses while the pointer is over the carousel or focus is inside
it, and stops for good with the play/pause button (first in tab order). With
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it waits until you press play.
