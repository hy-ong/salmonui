---
title: Accent and case matching
description: Search and filter-row matching with `matchCase` and `ignoreAccents`, so "rene" finds "Renée" and "zoe" finds "ZOE" and "Zoë".
---

Type in the search box or a filter-row cell and toggle the options: with
`ignoreAccents` diacritics are folded before comparing, and without
`matchCase` the comparison ignores case.
