title: Circular gauge with ranges and a draggable pointer
description: A speedometer with tapered ranges, minor ticks, an annotation, a range legend and tooltips, next to a controlled dial whose needle you drag or step with the keyboard.

Drag the second needle, or focus it and use the arrow keys, Page Up / Page
Down and Home / End. The legend buttons hide and show ranges.
