---
title: Breadcrumbs overflow
description: Every overflow mode on a resizable trail, with and without maxItems.
category: navigation
---

With `maxItems` unset, `overflow="menu"` measures the trail: narrow the box
and the items after the first collapse into the ellipsis menu. With
`maxItems`, the collapse is fixed (the same on the server). `expand` reveals
the hidden items inline; `wrap`, `scroll` and `none` never collapse.
