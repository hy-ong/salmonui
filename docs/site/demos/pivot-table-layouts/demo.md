---
title: Compact and tabular layouts
description: Switch a three-level row report between the compact treegrid and the tabular grid, drill members down and up, and expand or collapse every member.
category: pivot-table
---

Click a member's toggle, or focus a row header and press Enter or Space.
Ctrl+ArrowDown expands and Ctrl+ArrowUp collapses every row member. The
status line shows the last `onExpandedChange` event and its source.
