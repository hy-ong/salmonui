title: Button tones, variants and sizes
description: Every tone and variant (the contrast matrix), the three sizes, icons, an icon-only button with its name and tooltip, a disabled button, keyboard activation and a link rendered through `render`.
category: buttons
---

Tab through the buttons: each shows the `:focus-visible` ring, and hovering or
pressing changes its fill. Enter or Space on **Add** counts like a click. The
icon-only button is named "Add" (`aria-label`) and shows the native `title`
tooltip on hover; the disabled button is skipped by Tab. Switch the site color
scheme to check every tone and variant pair in dark mode.
