---
title: Pinned rows
description: Rows pinned by `isRowPinned` and at runtime with `api.pinRows` / `api.unpinRows` stay in the sticky top zone of 2,000 virtualized orders, follow sorting, filtering and search, and stay outside `pageSize`.
---

Three High-priority orders are pinned by `isRowPinned`. Select rows with the
checkboxes and pin or unpin them. Sort a column: the pinned rows reorder
among themselves. Filter Priority to `Low`: the pinned High orders hide
until the filter is cleared. Turn on paging: every page shows the pinned
rows above its 10 rows.
