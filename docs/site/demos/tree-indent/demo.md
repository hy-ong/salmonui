---
title: Tree drag and indent
description: Flat tree rows reordered by drag (before, after or into a row), the indent and outdent toolbar items, methods and keys, new rows above, below or as a child of the focused row, and subtree delete.
---

Dragging a parent moves its subtree; a row cannot drop into its own
descendant. Selection is not required to drag, and the drop event reports
the position and the new parent.
